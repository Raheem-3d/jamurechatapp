# JamureChat - User Research Questions (30 Questions)

## Section 1: Task Management (Q1-Q5)

1. **Task Creation Process** 
   - Aap apne team ko task kaise assign karte ho? Kya aap one-click se task bana sakte ho ya detailed form bharna padta hai?

2. **Task Deadline & Priority**
   - Task create karte samay kaun-kaun fields mandatory honi chahiye? (Title, Description, Deadline, Priority, Assignee)

3. **Task Assignment**
   - Ek saath kitne employees ko task assign kar sakte ho? Kya group assignment possible hona chahiye?

4. **Task Tracking**
   - Aap task ke progress ko kaise track karte ho - notifications se ya dashboard se?

5. **Task Completion & Status**
   - Task complete hone par kya notification mile? Aur kya pending/in-progress/completed status visible hona chahiye?

---

## Section 2: Employee Management (Q6-Q10)

6. **Employee Addition**
   - Naye employee ko add karte samay kya information chahiye? (Email, Phone, Department, Designation, etc.)

7. **Bulk Employee Import**
   - Kya aap multiple employees ko Excel/CSV se import karna pasand karoge?

8. **Employee Roles & Permissions**
   - Kaun-kaun se employee roles hone chahiye? (Admin, Manager, Team Lead, Staff, Viewer?)

9. **Employee Directory**
   - Kya employees ki complete directory/list dekhne ka option hona chahiye?

10. **Employee Activity**
    - Kya aap dekhna chahte ho ki kaunsa employee kitna active hai (last login, message count, etc.)?

---

## Section 3: Channel Management (Q11-Q15)

11. **Channel Creation**
    - Channel create karte samay kaun-kaun fields honi chahiye? (Name, Description, Type, Members?)

12. **Channel Types**
    - Kaun-kaun se channel types support hone chahiye? (Public, Private, Archived, Department-wise?)

13. **Channel Members**
    - Channel me members add/remove karte samay kya process hona chahiye? (Admin approval ya direct add?)

14. **Channel Privacy**
    - Kya aapko channels ke liye different privacy levels chahiye?

15. **Channel Description & Topics**
    - Kya har channel ke liye description/topic hona chahiye taaki purpose clear ho?

---

## Section 4: Communication & Messaging (Q16-Q20)

16. **Message Notifications**
    - Message notification ke liye kaun-kaun options hone chahiye? (Email, In-app, Sound, Desktop?)

17. **Message Pinning**
    - Pinned messages kite time tak visible reh sakte ho? Aur search me pin karte samay kya info visible hona chahiye?

18. **Message Search**
    - Message search me kaun-kaun filters hone chahiye? (Date, Sender, Keywords, Channels?)

19. **AI Message Rewriter**
    - AI rewriter ke liye kaun-kaun tones hone chahiye? (Professional, Casual, Formal, Friendly, Urgent, etc.)

20. **Message Threading**
    - Kya task-based thread structure hona chahiye ya simple message-reply?

---

## Section 5: Direct Messages (Q21-Q23)

21. **Personal Chat**
    - Kya direct message karte samay conversation history save honi chahiye?

22. **DM Notifications**
    - Direct message aane par immediate notification hona chahiye?

23. **DM Organization**
    - Kya recent contacts/favorites organize karne ka option chahiye?

## Section 6: Admin & Settings (Q24-Q27)

24. **Admin Dashboard**
    - Admin ko kya-kya information visible hona chahiye? (User count, Active users, Total tasks, etc.)

25. **Organization Settings**
    - Kya organization name, logo, domain customize karne ka option hona chahiye?

26. **Notification Preferences**
    - Kaun-kaun se notification preference settings honi chahiye? (Mute channels, Quiet hours, DND mode?)

27. **Profile Customization**
    - Employee profile me kaun-kaun fields hone chahiye? (Name, Email, Phone, Department, Bio, Avatar, Status?)

## Section 7: Subscription & Payment (Q28-Q30)

28. **Trial Period**
    - Free trial period kitne din ka hona chahiye? (7, 14, 30 days?)

29. **Subscription Plans**
    - Subscription plans me kaun-kaun features per tier hone chahiye? (Users, Storage, Support, etc.)

30. **Billing & Invoices**
    - Invoice format aur billing cycle kaisa hona chahiye? (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual?)
