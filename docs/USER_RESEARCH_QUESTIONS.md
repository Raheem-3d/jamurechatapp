## 🎬 **INTERVIEW FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS**

After each main question, use these follow-ups:

1. **Why?** - "इसलिए क्यों?"
2. **Example?** - "कोई उदाहरण दे सकते हो?"
3. **Alternative?** - "आप क्या prefer करोगे?"
4. **Impact?** - "इसका आपके काम पर क्या असर पड़ा?"
5. **Frequency?** - "कितनी बार use करते हो?"

---

## 💡 **PRO TIPS FOR CONDUCTING SURVEY**

✅ **Multi-language support** - Questions in English, Hinglish & Hindi
✅ **Mix question types** - Avoid only yes/no questions
✅ **Keep it concise** - 20-30 mins max interview time
✅ **Listen more, ask less** - Let users elaborate
✅ **Record feedback** - Take notes of specific use cases
✅ **Prioritize insights** - Group similar feedback together
✅ **Share results** - Show users that their feedback matters
